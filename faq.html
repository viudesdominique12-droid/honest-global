The group

What is Honest Global PLC?

Honest Global PLC is a group based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. It brings together three businesses: Honest Global Trading (imports, and Ethiopian coffee exports), Honest Oil (cooking oil) and Luxury Automobiles (vehicles sourced on demand).

How can we contact you?

By phone or WhatsApp on +251 911 204 388, by email at info@ethiopianfilmoffice.com, or through the contact page. Our office is in front of Meskel Square, Finfine Building, 5th Floor, Addis Ababa.

Imports

What products can you import?

We explore sourcing opportunities across 25 sectors, from industrial machinery and construction materials to electronics, medical equipment and food products. See all sectors.

Are the listed products in stock?

No. The sectors describe what we can look for, not stock. Products are available for sourcing on request, subject to availability and applicable import regulations.

My product is not listed. Can I still ask?

Yes. Send a sourcing request with the product name, preferred brand, specifications, quantity and, if you wish, a budget range. Our team assesses the sourcing possibilities, supplier availability and import requirements.

Do you import medicines, chemicals or other regulated products?

Only where the law allows it. Regulated products are sourced in line with Ethiopian registration, licensing and import requirements, and offered only where the company holds the necessary authorization.

Coffee exports

Do you export anything other than coffee?

No. Our only export is Ethiopian coffee.

How do I request a coffee quotation?

Fill in the coffee export quotation form with your destination country, estimated volume, specifications, packaging preferences and shipment timing. You can also ask for samples.

Where can I see grades and specifications?

Specifications such as origin, variety, processing method and grade are published only once they are confirmed for each coffee. Ask us through the quotation form.

Honest Oil

What is Honest Oil?

Honest Oil is a cooking oil brand, presented in a 5 L container.

Where are the product specifications?

They will be published once verified. In the meantime, request product information.

Luxury Automobiles

Are you an authorized car dealer?

No. Honest Global PLC is not an authorized dealer for the manufacturers shown on this website, and no partnership or endorsement is implied. We source vehicles on request.

Can I ask for a marque that is not listed?

Yes. The list only shows examples. Type any marque in the vehicle request form.

Can any vehicle be imported into Ethiopia?

Not necessarily. Each request is assessed against Ethiopian customs, tax, registration and import requirements, and we verify the vehicle and its configuration before confirming feasibility.

Are the cars in the photographs for sale?

No. The photographs are for illustration only and do not show vehicles available for purchase.
